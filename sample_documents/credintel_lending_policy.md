# CredIntel AI Comprehensive Underwriting & Loan Policy Standards
Document ID: POL-2026-V1.0
Classification: Operational Credit Standards

## 1. Executive Summary & Lending Principles
CredIntel operates an AI-assisted credit decisioning framework designed to assess borrower creditworthiness, mitigate systemic default exposure, and ensure equitable lending across retail and commercial portfolios. All automated risk predictions augment human underwriting scrutiny.

## 2. Core Eligibility Criteria
- **Age**: Minimum borrower age is 18 years; maximum age at loan maturity is 70 years.
- **Credit Amount Limits**: Minimum unsecured credit is 500 DM (or equivalent currency unit); maximum standard automated credit is 200,000 DM.
- **Loan Duration**: Term spans from 1 month to 72 months (6 years). Terms exceeding 48 months require Tier 1 credit score or real estate collateral.
- **Employment Stability**: Minimum continuous employment of 1 year is preferred. Applicants with < 1 year or unemployed status must have existing checking account balance ≥ 200 DM or a designated guarantor.

## 3. Risk Tiers & Default Probability Categorization
The machine learning scoring model assigns probability of default (PD):
- **LOW RISK (PD < 40%)**: Recommended for expedited automated approval. Standard interest rates apply.
- **MEDIUM RISK (40% ≤ PD < 70%)**: Requires secondary manual underwriting review. Compensating factors (e.g., guarantor, unencumbered real estate, checking balance > 200 DM) may allow conditional approval.
- **HIGH RISK (PD ≥ 70%)**: Elevated default propensity. Approval requires senior credit committee exception and verified primary collateral.

## 4. Installment Rate & Debt-Service Limits
- **Installment Rate 1 (≥ 35% of disposable income)**: Highest debt burden; strictly restricted to Low Risk borrowers with verified reserves.
- **Installment Rate 2 (25% - 35%)**: Acceptable for standard retail personal, auto, or home improvement loans.
- **Installment Rate 3 (20% - 25%)**: Optimal lending band; favorable term flexibility.
- **Installment Rate 4 (< 20%)**: Lowest installment pressure; eligible for preferential interest rate discounts.

## 5. Collateral & Guarantor Guidelines
- **Real Estate (Property Tier A121)**: Provides highest credit enhancement, reducing required loan loss provisioning.
- **Savings & Securities (Tier A122)**: Building society deposits and pledged securities qualify as secondary repayment sources.
- **Guarantors & Co-Applicants**: A solvent guarantor (A102 / A103) elevates a Medium Risk profile into approved status by legally pledging joint liability.

## 6. Credit History Evaluation
- **A30 / A31**: Flawless repayment record. Immediate qualification for prime terms.
- **A32**: Existing credits duly serviced. Standard qualification.
- **A33 / A34**: History of arrears or critical restructuring. Requires minimum 20% down payment or supplementary collateral.
